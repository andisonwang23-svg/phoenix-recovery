## Pre-drop safety checklist

Do not drop until every required box is checked.

- [ ] Drop zone is clear.
- [ ] No person is under the test article.
- [ ] Landing area is soft.
- [ ] Test article will not land on concrete, pavement, tile, stairs, or furniture.
- [ ] Camera is recording from a safe location.
- [ ] Payload cartridge cannot slide.
- [ ] Payload cartridge cannot twist.
- [ ] Payload cartridge does not rattle hard.
- [ ] Battery or dummy battery is strapped into the tray.
- [ ] Battery tray has hard stops at both ends.
- [ ] Smooth cover is installed and not squeezing the battery.
- [ ] No sharp screw tip, printed edge, servo horn, or wire can touch the battery.
- [ ] Battery lead has strain relief.
- [ ] Parafoil lines are separated from wires and servo arms.
- [ ] Servo arms cannot hit the battery or payload wall.
- [ ] Switch is reachable.
- [ ] Retention pins or screws are fully installed.
- [ ] Parafoil can be pulled out by hand without snagging.

## Post-drop inspection

Check after the test article is fully stopped and powered off.

- [ ] Battery pouch has no cuts, dents, swelling, heat, smell, or rubbing marks.
- [ ] Battery connector and wires are undamaged.
- [ ] Battery tray is not cracked.
- [ ] Smooth cover is not cracked and has no sharp edges.
- [ ] Strap is still tight.
- [ ] Hard stops are intact.
- [ ] Retention pins/screws are not loose.
- [ ] Payload rails and centering plates are not cracked.
- [ ] Servo mounts are secure.
- [ ] Servo arms are clear of wires, lines, and battery area.
- [ ] Parafoil lines have no fuzzing, cuts, knots, or snag marks.
- [ ] Wires and connectors are still strain relieved.
- [ ] Switch still works and is reachable.
- [ ] Log can be read, if powered.
